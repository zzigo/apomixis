# Division identity and historical names

Visible HYLON identity is **P · I · II · III · IV**, with one progressive hue palette defined in `packages/hylon-midi/divisions.js`: P40°, I95°, II150°, III205°, IV260°, saturation78%, lightness72%. Names remain in tooltips. Scene source IDs `pe/go/po/re/so`, genetic grid order and fixed MIDI channels remain unchanged. Genotype cells may vary brightness while retaining their division hue. Footage/family colours on individual stops are a distinct notation; output-route colours K/LA/MO are also distinct.

| UI | Existing score ID | MIDI channel | Historical name |
|---|---|---:|---|
| P | pe | 5 | Pédale |
| I | go | 1 | Grand-Orgue |
| II | po | 2 | Positif |
| III | re | 3 | Récit expressif |
| IV | so | 4 | Solo |

Positif derives from the instrument that could be set down, in contrast to being carried while played; its name passed to a subsidiary organ division. Source: [Académie française](https://www.dictionnaire-academie.fr/article/A9P3573). The classical Récit carried solo melody and developed into the larger expressive division of the19th century: [Inventaire national des orgues](https://inventaire-des-orgues.fr/lexique/). Grand-Orgue means the principal division: [Larousse](https://www.larousse.fr/dictionnaires/francais/grand-orgue/37857). Independent Pédale has its own stops played by the feet: [Brussels organ inventory](https://orgues.irisnet.be/fr/Glossaire/35/app.rvb).

## Piteå comparison

The intended comparison is Organ Acusticum in Studio Acusticum, Woehl, inaugurated2012, not the church organ elsewhere in Piteå. Donald Knuth's visit notes (2017) describe Hauptwerk/I, Positiv/II, Récit/III, Solo/IV, Pedal, plus Cornetwerk without a dedicated manual. Positiv is oriented towards central German timbres; Récit towards French; Hauptwerk and Pedal combine traditions. Source: [Knuth, Organ Acusticum](https://www-cs-faculty.stanford.edu/~knuth/oa.html).

The university describes approximately9000pipes, experiments and Internet-based performance: [LTU University Organ](https://www.ltu.se/en/about-the-university/organisation/school-of-music/university-organ). Klais Buenos Aires has four manuals and approximately3600pipes: [Argentina cultural authority](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/el-palacio-libertad-recupera-el-organo-klais-opus-1912-uno-de-los-instrumentos-mas). Shared manual numbering is a UI correspondence, not tonal, stop-number or MIDI/SysEx equivalence. Knuth's discussion of MIDI reflects2017 and is not a current control specification; no Piteå protocol was implemented in HYLON.
